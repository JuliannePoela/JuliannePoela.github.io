THE PERSON BEHIND IT

More Than
What I Create

I'm Julianne Poela Pascual, a 2nd year Multimedia Arts student who has been interested in art for as long as I can remember. Growing up, I always enjoyed drawing, creating things, and getting involved in creative activities. Over the years, that interest grew into something bigger as I started exploring filmmaking, graphic design, photography, and other forms of art.

VISUAL STORYTELLING   •   CREATIVE CONCEPTS   •   BRANDING   •   ART DIRECTION

Some of the areas I've explored include:

  • Graphic Design & Branding
  • Film Directing & Production
  • Photography
  • Digital & Traditional Illustration
  • Video Editing
  • 3D Modeling

“You don’t have to be good at everything to try everything.”

In the future, I hope to become a creative director, whether in filmmaking, advertising, branding, or content production. I've always enjoyed coming up with ideas and being involved in how they come to life. While I'm still figuring out which direction I want to focus on, I want to continue learning, improving my skills, and gaining more experience in different creative fields.

- Poe Pascual
